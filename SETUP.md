# Use the app

Open https://expenses-tracker-fec08.web.app. If an old version appears, tap Update when offered, or close the app and reopen. Do not clear browser data to update.

## Connect your devices once

1. On the device with the expense records you want to keep, open Data and export a backup.
2. Choose Sync this database and wait until connected.
3. Copy the private connection link and open it on your other device, or paste it into the connection field in Data.
4. Review the replacement notice, tick the confirmation and choose Connect device. Its old local data is retained as a recovery backup.
5. Repeat for your other browsers/devices using the same link. Do not create a separate database on each device.

Keep the link private: anyone with it can read and change your expenses. Connected saves need internet. The app has no sign-in and cannot recover a lost key; keep both the link and exported backups somewhere safe.

On iPhone, use Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android, use Chrome → Install app. Connect from the installed app if it uses separate storage.

## Daily entry

Use Quick amount, Add items, Scan receipt, or Expenses → Add several expenses. Repeat copies an existing purchase to today for editing. Bulk entry accepts date, shop, NZD amount and optional category separated by pipes or spreadsheet tabs; review before saving.

All amounts entered are NZD. The NZD/USD toolbar changes only displayed totals. Rates are daily reference rates; the displayed date and cached status show freshness.

For two 500 g packs costing $4 each, enter price 4, quantity 2, size 500 and measure g. For 0.75 kg sold at $12/kg, enter price 12, quantity 0.75, size 1 and measure kg. Discount applies to the entire line: 2 or −2 both deduct $2. Receipt-level adjustments are signed: negative subtracts, positive adds.

Select a suggested saved product to reuse it across shops. New items are remembered only when saving an expense. Products lets you edit names/aliases, merge duplicates, compare prices and remove catalog entries without removing past purchases.

Set, edit or remove the recurring weekly limit on Overview. Each week runs Monday through Sunday, including weeks spanning two months.

## Check prices while shopping

Open Products → Check a price → Live camera. Allow camera access and point at one clear shelf label. The app repeatedly reads frames on the device and shows an editable product, price and pack size. You can also use Photo or type manually. Stop the camera to adjust fields; nothing is saved as an expense from this screen.

Choose the correct product and any ambiguous price. Comparisons use compatible units and your previous purchases: orange is over 5% above your average, green is over 5% below. No history means no reliable cheaper/higher judgment yet. Product price history also supports comparison with a similar product.

Receipt photos use the same free local reader. First use downloads its assets. Review and reconcile the total before saving. Photos are not stored or uploaded.

## Backups

Data → Export backup saves the full database. Restore replaces the current database, including on connected devices. Export CSV from reports for a spreadsheet. Deleted expenses can be undone from Data until reload or another deletion.

## Improve a difficult receipt scan

After selecting a photo, adjust Left, Right, Top and Bottom to put only the receipt inside the green box, then choose Read selected area. Keep the product descriptions, price column and final total included. The reader straightens text and tries an alternate layout when needed. For best results, photograph the receipt flat and close enough to fill the frame. Review any total mismatch rather than adding an adjustment to hide unread items.

Camera comparison shows the current OCR result over the live preview. Put one shelf label inside the white target box and hold steady; scans repeat after each recognition finishes. Uploaded photos keep a static preview: tap a label to read that part of the photo. Multiple prices require selection. Confirm names and pack sizes before adding an unknown product with Add product to database. Adding creates the reusable product; price history is based on saved purchases, so it cannot judge a new product until comparable purchase history exists. Small or blurred shelf text may need a closer photo or manual correction.
